  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:04:24.68" data-timestamp="264.68">OK, so instead of four different acts today, what we're going to do is we're going to present the show as four questions. Tobin will explain more.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Tobin Low</h4><p begin="00:05:00.02" data-timestamp="300.02"> The conversation was about the questions people ask on first dates, the kind that force who someone really is out into the open, maybe even without them realizing. Emanuele, our executive editor, asks it this way.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Emanuele Berry</h4><p begin="00:05:21.21" data-timestamp="321.21"> "What do you think of Beyoncé?" It's like a red flag, basically.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Bim Adewunmi</h4><p begin="00:05:55.50" data-timestamp="355.5"> If they describe her singing style as kind of like caterwauling or like, ugh, she's just screeching--</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Tobin Low</h4><p begin="00:06:01.51" data-timestamp="361.51"> Bim, another producer, has also asked this question on many a date.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Bim Adewunmi</h4><p begin="00:06:05.48" data-timestamp="365.48"> All these words that have double meanings if you're a woman, and also if you're a Black woman-- I'm just like, all right, so you don't like loud people. OK. OK. That means you don't like me.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Tobin Low</h4><p begin="00:06:16.67" data-timestamp="376.67"> Could you tell me about some specific times that you've asked the Beyoncé question, and what the guy's response was and what it told you?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Emanuele Berry</h4><p begin="00:06:25.37" data-timestamp="385.37"> First date, bar date, pretty standard. I was like, oh, well, I really like her, and like, I don't really think I'm in a cult.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Tobin  Low</h4><p begin="00:06:50.24" data-timestamp="410.24"> Emanuele walks the guy through all the reasons Beyoncé is, in fact, pretty great.
