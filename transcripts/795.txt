  <h3>Act One: First Trimester</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:05:30.97" data-timestamp="330.97"> First trimester. And for some people, that's it. They won't get abortions. But others, of course, like Kae and Taylor, they're still going to try to get one. And for them, the state bans are a hurdle that slows things down as they try to figure out how.</p><p begin="00:06:00.10" data-timestamp="360.1">The anti-abortion movement is aware that there's this new window of time where maybe they could reach these people and persuade them to change their minds. Since the fall of <i>Roe,</i> there's been a big push to do just that, to increase state funding for crisis pregnancy centers, which already get millions of dollars from taxpayers. These are clinics whose whole mission is to convince people to not get abortions.</p><p begin="00:06:22.24" data-timestamp="382.24">Meanwhile, online, anti-abortion folks are trying to connect with people who are looking for information about abortions. An anti-abortion activist reached both Kae and Taylor during the first trimesters. And each responded very differently. Caroline Kitchener watched this unfold for each of them in two separate states, week by week.</p><p begin="00:06:41.70" data-timestamp="401.7">Just a quick note that we've changed some names in today's show for people who requested that for privacy, though Kae and Taylor are their real names. Anyway, here's Caroline.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Caroline Kitchener</h4><p begin="00:06:51.35" data-timestamp="411.35"> Kae was in Ohio. Taylor was in Georgia. Both states had just banned abortion after six weeks of pregnancy. That leaves a super short window to get an abortion.</p><p begin="00:07:03.32" data-timestamp="423.32">From the beginning, Kate tried to approach her pregnancy the way she approaches other things in her life. She's methodical, organized. Kae is 25 years old, white, owns her own pet-sitting business.</p><p begin="00:07:15.44" data-timestamp="435.44">She kept track of her periods on an app. And when she missed one, she took a pregnancy test. It was positive. She started calling clinics to try to get an appointment for an abortion. Kae wasn't completely sure she wanted one. Ever since that walk with her boyfriend to the Dairy Queen, she'd started going back and forth, wondering if maybe she should keep the pregnancy after all. She made a pro/con list. She really wasn't sure.</p><p begin="00:07:40.85" data-timestamp="460.85">Her boyfriend kept telling her that he'd support her no matter what. That wasn't exactly helpful. She pressed him, trying to figure out what he actually thought.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Kae</h4><p begin="00:07:49.73" data-timestamp="469.73"> Because I was like, well, OK, I'm making up my mind.
