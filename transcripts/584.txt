  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:00:00.35" data-timestamp="0.35"> You may remember this story that we did about a year ago, about canvassers going door to door and talking to voters for 20 minutes. Abortion.</p><p begin="00:00:16.89" data-timestamp="16.89">Which, of course, is amazing, right? That is not something that seems like it's even possible. There was a study in a well-known, peer reviewed journal-- <i>Science</i> magazine-- proving how remarkably effective these people were. They invented a whole new way to sway voter opinion. It seemed like a game changer, a whole new tool for politics, for liberals or conservatives to use.</p><p begin="00:00:37.06" data-timestamp="37.06">And then a month after our radio story-- maybe you saw this in the news-- there was overwhelming evidence that one of the researchers, a grad student named Michael LaCour had fabricated all the data. <i>Science</i> magazine retracted the article.</p><p begin="00:00:52.05" data-timestamp="52.05">But the team that invented this canvassing technique, they were not the ones that did the research study. That was a separate thing. And they were just as shocked as anybody. Maybe more shocked.</p><p begin="00:01:00.82" data-timestamp="60.82">The week the news broke, one of the canvass organizers, Steve DeLine, told me they'd spent months meticulously going door to door and keeping perfect records of everything they were doing for this researcher. They happened to be in town from California. And they had news.</p><p begin="00:01:54.43" data-timestamp="114.43">A brand new study with new researchers was looking into their work. Results were coming in. Here's Dave.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Dave Fleischer</h4><p begin="00:01:59.18" data-timestamp="119.18"> We're not anticipating going public with these results until January at the earliest, and maybe not till June.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:02:07.59" data-timestamp="127.59"> What these researchers really wanted to do was publish their results in <i>Science</i> magazine, you know, the place that published the original, discredited study. [LAUGHS] Well, no [BEEP] fooling.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:02:36.68" data-timestamp="156.68"> Well, apparently <i>Science</i> magazine liked what the new researchers did. [LAUGHS]</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Canvasser</h4><p begin="00:05:15.09" data-timestamp="315.09">Oh!
