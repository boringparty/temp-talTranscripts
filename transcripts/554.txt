Was it some kind of scam?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Adriana Cardona-maguigad</h4><p begin="00:06:06.73" data-timestamp="366.73">For example, is anybody getting their IDs and selling them in the black market? Is anybody making money out of this?</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:06:26.83" data-timestamp="386.83">She talked to a case manager who works with injection drug users in Chicago.
