  <h3>Act One: Act One</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:05:30.62" data-timestamp="330.62"> It's <i>This American Life.</i> So like I was saying before the break, for months, no judge would talk to us. Here she is.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Nadia Reiman</h4><p begin="00:05:45.67" data-timestamp="345.67"> It takes a very specific person to be an immigration judge, the kind that likes minutia, likes listening to hours of translations from Quiché to Spanish to English, just to get someone to confirm their mailing address. And obviously, you have to believe in rules. One main rule they have-- don't talk to the press.</p><p begin="00:06:06.46" data-timestamp="366.46">I ended up talking to 15 judges from all over the country. Desperate times, I guess. George Pappas was one of them. He works at a court in Chelmsford, Massachusetts, close to Boston-- a pretty new judge. He's been on the bench about two years.</p><p begin="00:06:22.74" data-timestamp="382.74">An average day for him until recently was usually one of two kinds of hearings-- either a lot of quick, repetitive, boring hearings just to schedule the next hearing or a full asylum case.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>George Pappas</h4><p begin="00:06:35.28" data-timestamp="395.28"> There were some cases that I could not grant, so I'll go through those, OK?</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Nadia Reiman</h4><p begin="00:06:39.16" data-timestamp="399.16"> Sure.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>George Pappas</h4><p begin="00:06:39.22" data-timestamp="399.22"> Let me go through those first.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Nadia Reiman</h4><p begin="00:06:40.12" data-timestamp="400.12"> In those asylum hearings, a person would have to make the case that they cannot live anywhere in their home country because they're being persecuted due to their race, religion, nationality, political opinion, or the ever so squishy membership in a particular social group.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>George Pappas</h4><p begin="00:06:56.20" data-timestamp="416.2"> I've seen a lot of cases out of Brazil where the immigrant is saying, I'm being persecuted, and I fear for my life because the loan shark that I borrowed money from is after me.
