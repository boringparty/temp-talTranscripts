A month or two after he'd passed on, she asked this son what Jazz was, and when he told her it was a kind of music, she knew immediately that he was telling the truth.</p><p begin="00:55:48.74" data-timestamp="3348.74">&quot;Is it bad music?&quot; she asked.</p><p begin="00:55:51.83" data-timestamp="3351.83">&quot;Well, if it's played badly,&quot; he said. &quot;Otherwise, it's really quite pleasant.&quot;</p><p begin="00:55:57.48" data-timestamp="3357.48">&quot;Did squirrels invent it?&quot;</p><p begin="00:55:59.49" data-timestamp="3359.49">&quot;God, no,&quot; he said. &quot;Whoever gave you that idea?&quot;</p><p begin="00:56:03.39" data-timestamp="3363.39">The chipmunk stroked her brown and white muzzle. &quot;Nobody,&quot; she said. &quot;I was just guessing.&quot;</p><p begin="00:56:20.24" data-timestamp="3380.24">When her muzzle grew more white than brown, the chipmunk forgot that she and the squirrel had had nothing to talk about.
