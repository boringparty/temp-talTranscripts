And one summer night around the campfire he and his friends started talking about all these supernatural things they had heard of.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>David Maxon</h4><p begin="00:00:20.37" data-timestamp="20.37"> Not just ghost stories, but have you ever used a Ouija board? And then the next thing I remember is we all went to sleep. And I woke up in the middle of the night. I don't know if I was half in a dream, or what. And in my mind-- with all of the conversation that had gone on earlier in the evening-- I thought it was the devil, that all this talk had somehow opened up some door, like if you use a Ouija board you can bring spirits.</p><p begin="00:01:28.60" data-timestamp="88.6">And I knew my friend had a crucifix that he had brought with him, this sort of three inch metal crucifix. David thought, OK, that worked. And he got back to his cabin, and changed his clothes for lunch. And he saw the guy who he had borrowed the crucifix from the night before, whose name was Joe.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>David Maxon</h4><p begin="00:03:23.05" data-timestamp="203.05"> And I said to Joe, do you want me to wait for you to go to lunch? That's not what you taught me.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>David Maxon</h4><p begin="00:06:40.53" data-timestamp="400.53"> Yeah, it's kind of like after I talked with the priest, I thought, don't you believe this, dude? These people are haunted by the role they played in somebody's death.</p><p begin="00:09:26.28" data-timestamp="566.28">And even in situations where everyone says they're blameless, where they seem blameless, they have a hard time feeling blameless.
