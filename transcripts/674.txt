But not as nice as, I'd never thought about this before and you really changed the way I think about the way our field is working.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Lilly Sullivan</h4><p begin="00:53:50.28" data-timestamp="3230.28"> Malcolm finally got his findings in front of the people who skipped his talk, the vertebrate majority, when he submitted his research for publication.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Malcolm Rosenthal</h4><p begin="00:53:57.63" data-timestamp="3237.63"> OK, yeah, the title of the paper is <i>Taxonomic</i> <i>Bias</i> <i>in</i> <i>Animal</i> <i>Behaviour</i> <i>Publications.</i> Evidence suggests that certain taxonomic groups are more thoroughly studied than others.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Lilly Sullivan</h4><p begin="00:54:10.24" data-timestamp="3250.24"> The way scientific journals usually work is that when they consider a paper, they send it out for peer review. Basically, they get a bunch of other scientists in the field to look at it. It's all anonymous. When Malcolm gets back his peer reviews, they're unlike any he's gotten in the past. For one thing, they're twice the number of reviewers-- four when you usually get two. What's their response like?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Malcolm Rosenthal</h4><p begin="00:54:33.97" data-timestamp="3273.97"> The response was mixed, I guess, would be one way to put it. But mixed maybe doesn't explain how intense the extremes of the mixture were.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Lilly Sullivan</h4><p begin="00:54:47.39" data-timestamp="3287.39"> Because some people were very into it and some people were very not into it?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Malcolm Rosenthal</h4><p begin="00:54:52.28" data-timestamp="3292.28"> Yeah. Yeah, people were angry.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Lilly Sullivan</h4><p begin="00:55:03.25" data-timestamp="3303.25"> And they went to a place he did not anticipate. I ask him to read me one of the negative reviews.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Malcolm Rosenthal</h4><p begin="00:55:08.59" data-timestamp="3308.59"> Ugh "The solution advocated by the authors of this study is affirmative action by journal editorial boards to promote and highlight work on underrepresented taxa. I'm getting all steamed just reading this again. Ugh.</p><p begin="00:55:47.90" data-timestamp="3347.9">I don't know. I mean, the idea that we were proposing a quota system is crazy and it also kind of just means that they didn't read the paper. It means they got mad. They read part of the paper, got mad, and then didn't read the rest. I mean, honestly it does remind me of the conversations around affirmative action. I guess maybe that's affirmative action. I don't think it is, though.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Jackie</h4><p begin="00:56:28.40" data-timestamp="3388.4"> It was weird. Like the way that people would be like, we only take the papers that are the best, and the bird ones happen to be the best.</p><p begin="00:56:54.87" data-timestamp="3414.87">And it's like, that was so similar to hearing about like, we just happen to take the best candidates. They're all white and dudes, but that doesn't mean anything. It's like, really? Does that not mean anything? Yeah. And I'm tired of talking about it.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Lilly Sullivan</h4><p begin="00:58:21.83" data-timestamp="3501.83"> She felt like, I know how this is going to end. He was fired up, like they were on the verge of a revolution and it was time to lead the resistance for the ignored creatures of the world-- the boneless, the slimy, the microscopic.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Malcolm Rosenthal</h4><p begin="00:58:37.92" data-timestamp="3517.92"> Like, the iron is hot. We've got the editorial staff at <i>Animal</i> <i>Behaviour</i> thinking about this. This is the time to strike.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Lilly Sullivan</h4><p begin="00:58:43.66" data-timestamp="3523.66"> And it seemed like it was working. He and two colleagues organized a special symposium. First, how does this bias limit what humans know about the animal world? And second, what should we do about it? Let's brainstorm solutions.</p><p begin="00:59:29.87" data-timestamp="3569.87">It was a big deal. It's exactly what Malcolm had wanted. But when it came time to start the big two part conversation, they began by asking the audience, who here works with vertebrates? Raise your hands.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Malcolm Rosenthal</h4><p begin="00:59:41.62" data-timestamp="3581.62"> And no one there did. And then in the end, nothing really changes.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Malcolm Rosenthal</h4><p begin="01:00:48.83" data-timestamp="3648.83"> Yeah, that's the perfect summary.
