  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:00:00.55" data-timestamp="0.55">When I was in my 20s, I worked as a temp typist on the overnight shift. And I'd take the elevator to the, you know, 20th floor or whatever it was. And it would be all lit up and bustling with activity, dozens of people rushing around. Usually we were hustling out some kind of legal brief or exhibit for court the next day.</p><p begin="00:00:24.98" data-timestamp="24.98">I loved being up when the rest of the world was asleep.
