I went to his farewell. And his parents were all about, &quot;Oh, he loves you so much. You're the greatest boss,&quot; yada, yada. Of course, you know how I took all of that. I'm just filing it in crazy town.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:48:28.87" data-timestamp="2908.87">The 19-year-old leaves. Benny stews for six months, driving his best friend Parker nuts with all of his obsessing. Parker, by the way, in this story, seems like the greatest guy in the world-- straight, Mormon, but understanding Benny better than Benny was understanding himself at the time. And it is with Parker, at 2:00 in the morning, that Benny comes up with his idea that is so crazy that it might just work.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Benny</h4><p begin="00:48:52.59" data-timestamp="2932.59">I said, &quot;We should go visit him in Peru.&quot; And Parker says, &quot;You know what, Benny, let's go. Let's go. Let's go visit him. Yeah, let's go see him.&quot; And I said, &quot;I'm serious. I will book the tickets tonight. And he said, &quot;OK.&quot;</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:49:08.34" data-timestamp="2948.34">Benny then goes to the 19-year-old's parents and says that he's taking a vacation to Peru. Would they want him to bring a package or something from the family, maybe? Seems like a nice offer. They say, of course. Everybody agrees to make it a surprise for the 19-year-old, more fun that way. So Benny and Parker fly to Peru, make their way to the remote mountain town where the 19-year-old was now a missionary. They wander around asking people, where is the Mormon church in town?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Benny</h4><p begin="00:49:31.70" data-timestamp="2971.7">I ended up-- someone said, &quot;Oh yes, we know where the Mormoni&quot; or whatever they called it. They said, &quot;Yeah, we know where the Mormons are.&quot; And so they told us where the chapel was. And we got to the chapel. And these elders were just, oh, my gosh. They're yelling through the church, &quot;Oh, my gosh. Buddy from Utah is here. And, oh, my gosh, you just showed up. Like, who does that? That's the coolest thing. Dude, how expensive were those tickets?&quot; It turns into to this big--</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:49:54.18" data-timestamp="2994.18">Did you feel guilty at all?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Benny</h4><p begin="00:49:55.35" data-timestamp="2995.35">Oh, my gosh. And I started to become very uncomfortable.</p><p begin="00:50:21.28" data-timestamp="3021.28">And then, all of a sudden, one of the elders came. They said, &quot;He's here.&quot; I felt as if I was going to puke. And then he came in. He walked right in. And when he walked in, he stopped in his tracks, and he's, &quot;No way.&quot; And he just dropped all of his books. And he just, &quot;What?&quot; And he ran over to me, and he just grabbed me and hugged me so tight. It was so tight and so-- And he was like, &quot;What are you doing here?&quot; And Parker is friends with him. So Parker's like, &quot;Hey, dude.&quot; Parker's going along with this ridiculous, cockamamie thing that I have done.</p><p begin="00:51:03.27" data-timestamp="3063.27">And he called his mission president or someone and said, &quot;I have friends from Salt Lake City that are traveling through Peru.&quot; He got permission or something to come and take time with us. And so it's, like, 7:30 at night now. It's getting dark.</p><p begin="00:51:16.91" data-timestamp="3076.91">And freakin' Parker, he's like, &quot;OK, I'm gonna--&quot; And if you know anything about missionaries, you can't be away from your companion that's with you, can't separate from each other, ever, unless you're going to the bathroom. So Parker's like, &quot;I'm gonna distract him enough, where we're in a conversation, where you can at least get a good 50 feet away from us, where he can still see you so it won't look weird. And you can spend some time with him and just talk to him, see how his mission's going.&quot;</p><p begin="00:51:44.57" data-timestamp="3104.57">So him and I sat on the steps of this cathedral. And I said, &quot;How's the mission going?&quot; And he started telling me. And then he just starts to cry. And then he says, &quot;I feel like you're an answer to my prayer, because I have been struggling so much here.&quot; My heart is ready to beat out of my bloody body. I mean, it's just--</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:52:03.70" data-timestamp="3123.7">This is your reaction.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Benny</h4><p begin="00:52:03.89" data-timestamp="3123.89">I remember the pulse that was happening. It almost hurt.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:52:08.15" data-timestamp="3128.15">Because he said that, you're the answer to my prayer.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Benny</h4><p begin="00:52:10.41" data-timestamp="3130.41">Yeah. And then he said, &quot;I've been struggling.&quot; I said, &quot;Listen. I've been on a mission. I know what that feels like. I have been here. I know how confusing and difficult and lonely it can be.&quot; And he said, &quot;I just can't believe you came here.&quot; And then he said, &quot;This has got to be the coolest thing anyone has ever done for me, to just come here and do something this, for you to come and see me.&quot; And then he said, &quot;Wow, Benny, I feel so much happiness seeing you. And you are that kind of person that has always filled me with joy and hope. And every time I'm around you, I want to be better.&quot;</p><p begin="00:52:47.02" data-timestamp="3167.02">And then I just said, &quot;Well, you know, I love you a lot.&quot; And he said, &quot;I love you, too.&quot; And then it was like Tourette's. I couldn't even help myself. And I just said, &quot;No. I love you.&quot; And then he kind of tilted his head. And he looked at me, and he said, &quot;I love you, too, bro.&quot; I saw in his face, the way he looked at me.</p><p begin="00:53:18.42" data-timestamp="3198.42">And then, my eyes are starting to fill up with tears. I'm starting to panic. All of this that I've held inside of me is crumbling. Quivering lip, the whole puppy face, I can't even imagine what he was seeing. And Parker-- I look over. Parker, I see him in the background with his hand like, &quot;cut it&quot; to his neck. He just was looking at me like, &quot;Benny, cut it.&quot; He was watching the body language. He could see what was unfolding. He was like, &quot;Benny, just cut it.&quot;</p><p begin="00:53:47.99" data-timestamp="3227.99">And actually, this guy, he knew I had deviated from &quot;I love you, bro.&quot; And then he's like, &quot;You know, we gotta go. It's getting really dark.&quot; And I was crushed, because in that moment, it was like I'd been asleep and someone had woken me up.</p><p begin="00:54:08.04" data-timestamp="3248.04">And all of a sudden-- we were walking away, and I said to Parker, &quot;He's straight, isn't he?&quot; And Parker said, &quot;Benny, yes. He is.&quot; And I just went crazy and started yelling at Parker. &quot;How could you let me do this? You were supposed to be my voice of reason! You're my best friend! I confided in you in this! This is your fault! You put these thoughts in my head!&quot; And I was so angry, I just started screaming at him in the middle of the street.</p><p begin="00:54:36.53" data-timestamp="3276.53">And I was bawling like a crazy person and just saying, &quot;What is this feeling?&quot; And he's like, &quot;Benny, you're feeling crushed, like you're heart's breaking.&quot; He's like, &quot;This is good. You're supposed to feel this. It's a little late.&quot; And I'm like, &quot;Don't tell me how to feel! I want to go home. I don't even want to stay in Peru anymore.&quot; And Parker just sat.</p><p begin="00:54:57.20" data-timestamp="3297.2">And I said, &quot;Tell me this. Have you known all along that this was going to happen?&quot; He goes, &quot;Well, to be honest, I didn't know you were going to do that. But of course I knew he was straight. I tried to tell you one time. You got a little weird about it. And I just decided in my head, Benny needs to experience this.&quot; I cried for hours. And Parker was just listening to me.</p><p begin="00:55:17.52" data-timestamp="3317.52">And then he was the one that said, &quot;You imagine, Benny, feeling this but having someone reciprocating it.&quot; And I said, &quot;You mean, with a gay guy?&quot; And he said, &quot;Well, yeah, because that's the only guy that's going to do that with you.&quot; And then it was this journey to coming out of the closet. That was what cracked it opened, you know?</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:55:47.50" data-timestamp="3347.5">Benny now lives in New York City.
