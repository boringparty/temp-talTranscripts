  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:00:00.31" data-timestamp="0.31"> It's one thing to go through something so horrible that you think you're gonna die. This time, that comforting ding ding did not occur, and we just weren't ascending at the normal rate.</p><p begin="00:00:58.65" data-timestamp="58.65">And the pilot got on. And he said, &quot;Well, folks.&quot; And you know, I have this joke that whenever a pilot says &quot;folks,&quot; it's never good news.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:01:10.78" data-timestamp="70.78">The pilot tells everybody that he's getting a signal that the landing gear won't retract. Sounded high-tech.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Alexandra Jacobs</h4><p begin="00:01:27.98" data-timestamp="87.98">But then he said that we were going to do a low flyby, and that people on the ground were going to inspect the underbelly of our plane with binoculars to see what was wrong with the landing gear. And I thought, my God. Like that's how they're going to, you know, check out the safety of the plane? With binoculars? Like the ones my husband uses for birdwatching? You know, it just didn't seem-- it seemed very retro, you know, to have someone peering at our plane.</p><p begin="00:01:59.89" data-timestamp="119.89">I don't think people began to sort of panic and cry and get upset until the words &quot;emergency landing&quot; were bandied about.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Pilot</h4><p begin="00:02:10.01" data-timestamp="130.01">We have run all the relevant checklists and are confident that we know exactly what the issue is, as well as the issues to be dealt with.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:02:19.28" data-timestamp="139.28">This is sound from a video camera that a passenger pulled out once it became clear that something unusual was happening on the flight.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Pilot</h4><p begin="00:02:25.29" data-timestamp="145.29">At this point, what I'm doing is burning off additional fuel.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Alexandra Jacobs</h4><p begin="00:02:28.49" data-timestamp="148.49">And almost simultaneously, or very close to that announcement, I looked a couple of rows in front of me, and saw that someone's television had imagery of a plane circling in a blue sky. I'm sorry about that. I apologize for any apprehension that this has caused to you. I want to assure you--</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Alexandra Jacobs</h4><p begin="00:03:17.10" data-timestamp="197.1">I mean, I think that ratcheted up the level of alarm, because it wasn't local news. It was national news. It was in the same text zipper as Hurricane Rita. It was on multiple channels. People were flipping around and they're like, oh my God, it's on FOX, it's on ABC. You know, and we're all familiar with these national events that take over your television, where you flip from channel to channel and it's the same image.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:03:42.26" data-timestamp="222.26">Right. You are in the white Bronco, all of a sudden.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Alexandra Jacobs</h4><p begin="00:03:44.82" data-timestamp="224.82">Exactly. It wasn't a good feeling.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:03:49.04" data-timestamp="229.04">For years, Alexandra has been having these conversations with her husband and his brother and his dad-- the brother and dad were actually Navy pilots-- about whether flying was safe. Alexandra, of course, said that it was not. And the thought went through her head right now that well, if we crash, at least I win that argument.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Alexandra Jacobs</h4><p begin="00:04:04.09" data-timestamp="244.09">I always knew I was going to perish in a plane crash, and you know, it's just coming true. Here it is.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:04:13.77" data-timestamp="253.77">Well, today on our radio program, stories of people who thought they were goners, and what they say once it's clear that they're back from the dead. Act Three. &quot;Friday Night Floodlights.&quot; A story of a town that is actually near that town in Act Two, which doesn't have a high school up and running yet, but all kinds of people have turned their lives upside down to restart high school football. Stay with us.</p></div>  </div>

<div class="act" id="act1">
  <h3>Act One: In the Event of An Emergency</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:04:59.39" data-timestamp="299.39">Act One. In the Event of an Emergency.</p><p begin="00:05:02.44" data-timestamp="302.44">So in addition to Alexandra Jacobs, I talked to two other passengers from that JetBlue flight.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Zach Mastoon</h4><p begin="00:05:06.49" data-timestamp="306.49">We basically had three hours total of flying in circles.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:05:11.47" data-timestamp="311.47">Zach Mastoon was sitting in the very back of the plane.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Zach Mastoon</h4><p begin="00:05:13.82" data-timestamp="313.82">It was terrifying. I quickly asked the stewardess for a drink. It never happened. I never got the drink. They didn't do any service. There was just too much going on. And I was seated next to this really nice guy. He's in real estate. His name was AJ. He was really my angel. And he offered me this large bottle of what looked like seltzer, and he said, hey, you can have a swig of this. It's leaded.</p><p begin="00:05:38.90" data-timestamp="338.9">So I started drinking this really strong vodka tonic. And it was brilliant. It really took the edge off, and allowed me to look at the situation in a much more accepting way? If I hadn't had something to drink-- I know that sounds sort of pathetic, but worrying, and you know, freaking out, watching the news and hearing commentators tell me that I could die in a ball of fire wasn't, you know, what I wanted to do. So I flipped on Comedy Central. Started watching <i>The</i> <i>Daily</i> <i>Show</i>.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Dave Reinitz</h4><p begin="00:06:08.16" data-timestamp="368.16">People were up and about more than I would have thought they would be. Just getting their stuff, or going to the bathroom, or you know, whatever.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:06:16.30" data-timestamp="376.3">Dave Reinitz is the guy who was shooting videotape on the flight, sitting in seat 18D.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Dave Reinitz</h4><p begin="00:06:20.57" data-timestamp="380.57">Yeah, people were-- there was some talking. But people, I think, were very in their own heads, and very-- trying to get their emotions in check for what was happening.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Zach Mastoon</h4><p begin="00:06:31.32" data-timestamp="391.32">Nobody was making a spectacle out of themselves, and no one was really, like, just going bananas.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Alexandra Jacobs</h4><p begin="00:06:37.06" data-timestamp="397.06">It was very tense. I mean, everyone was dealing with it in his or her individual style. You had the weepers. You know, it was later reported that grown men were crying, but I didn't observe any.
