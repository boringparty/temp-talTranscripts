  <h3>Act One: Life In A Bubble</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:04:43.66" data-timestamp="283.66">Act One, Life in a Bubble. But you might not expect to find these kinds of stories at the Toy Fair. The Toy Fair is the big trade show for the toy world. Thousands of manufacturers and buyers descend on New York City for the event every year. Our contributing editor Paul Tough went with Aaron Hsu-Flanders, a toy entrepreneur.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Paul Tough</h4><p begin="00:05:17.83" data-timestamp="317.83">Aaron and I are walking through the entrance hall of the Jacob Javits Center, just hanging out, talking when suddenly we see Amazing [? He's making balloon animals.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Amazing Shoney</h4><p begin="00:05:35.61" data-timestamp="335.61">You have a magical day now.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Paul Tough</h4><p begin="00:05:38.31" data-timestamp="338.31">Shoney hands Aaron a lopsided pink inflated giraffe. And the two of them just chat for a few minutes about some of the current issues in balloon sculpting.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Amazing Shoney</h4><p begin="00:06:06.21" data-timestamp="366.21">I haven't been able to make a good Barney. Can you make Barney? I make him a tyrannosaurus rex that's purple. It doesn't quite look like it. But it's enough to pass.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Paul Tough</h4><p begin="00:06:17.64" data-timestamp="377.64">We take our giraffe and walk away. Aaron tells me what he thinks.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Aaron Hsu-flanders</h4><p begin="00:06:20.95" data-timestamp="380.95">I wasn't blown away. He made a sort of generic-looking combination dog giraffe kind of thing that you couldn't-- it was more the power of suggestion. He was sort of a follower of the theory that all balloon animals are essentially dogs with a little variation here and there. Lacked a little of the finesse that I admit I have to sort of insist upon in order to be impressed with someone.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Paul Tough</h4><p begin="00:06:51.03" data-timestamp="411.03">Aaron's been making animals out of balloons for more than 20 years.
