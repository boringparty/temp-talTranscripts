I was mostly offended by it.</p><p begin="00:03:34.06" data-timestamp="214.06">So I figured that I should end that line of conversation as forcefully as possible and kind of shock her back. <div class="act" id="act1">
  <h3>Act One: Who's Really On Line 1?</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:07:51.36" data-timestamp="471.36">Act One, "Who's Really On Line Two?"
