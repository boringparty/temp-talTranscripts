  <h3>Act One: Take Your Kid to Work Day</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:06:01.97" data-timestamp="361.97"> Act One, Take Your Kid to Work Day.
