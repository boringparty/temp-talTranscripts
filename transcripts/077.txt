  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:05:27.36" data-timestamp="327.36"> Act One, Exodus. Usually we think of prayer as a private thing. All prayer like all politics is local. They use maps and computers and statistics to chart out what parts of their city need prayer for what reasons merged with something that cannot be less modern, prayer.</p><p begin="00:06:10.17" data-timestamp="370.17">And, when <i>This</i> <i>American</i> <i>Life</i> producer, Alix Spiegel, started to investigate it, it became something she could not get out of her mind. Then you do another block, and you make sure your buddy's there, and make sure your buddy's there. Then you pray another block. So you pray over every home, every business, every school, everything.
