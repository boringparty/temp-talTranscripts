  <h3>Act One: Jesus Has Prison</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:05:26.72" data-timestamp="326.72">Act One, Christ Has Prison.</p><p begin="00:05:36.85" data-timestamp="336.85">We were worried that was a little too Eastery. It's a Christmas show.</p><p begin="00:05:46.22" data-timestamp="346.22">Wyatt Cenac is a correspondent and a performer for <i>The</i> <i>Daily</i> <i>Show</i> <i>with</i> <i>Jon</i> <i>Stewart</i>. And he has this story about spreading Christmas cheer in Texas. Please welcome him.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Wyatt Cenac</h4><p begin="00:06:08.56" data-timestamp="368.56">Thank you. I am from Texas.</p><p begin="00:06:10.74" data-timestamp="370.74">[LAUGHTER]</p></div><div class="host"><p begin="00:06:12.60" data-timestamp="372.6">That's not normally the response I expect. Its very weird because when you tell somebody that you're from Texas, their response is, &quot;Oh my God, I love Austin!&quot; Which is cool, but I grew up in Dallas. And people will be like, &quot;Oh, I'm sorry. Austin's pretty awesome. Austin's great.&quot;</p><p begin="00:06:37.06" data-timestamp="397.06">It's weird. I've lived here in New York now for about two and a half years, and I was actually born here. I lived here as a baby. And it's nice to be back. The city has changed a lot since I lived here as a baby. When I was a baby, everywhere you went it was just colors and shapes everywhere. Gentrification has changed all of that. In my old neighborhood, I remember on the corner there was this amorphous yellow blob. Now it's a Chipotle.</p><p begin="00:07:12.42" data-timestamp="432.42">When my mother hit her 50s, she went through a bit of a midlife crisis. And typically, a midlife crisis is something that people associate with men. Men who are struggling with getting older, they maybe go out and buy themselves a Corvette or some other type of penis car. And my mother didn't do that. What she did for her midlife crisis was she went to church wearing those big fancy church lady hats that star in Tyler Perry movies.</p><p begin="00:07:46.25" data-timestamp="466.25">You see, a man in a midlife crisis maybe goes and gets himself an old Mustang and soups it up. And my mother did the same thing. She would take these hats, and you'd see her at church on Sunday with a bunch of other middle aged ladies. And they'd have tricked these hats out with lace and ribbons and flowers and dodo eggs, whatever they could get their hands on. They'd put rims and a spoiler on there if they could. And this is what my mother did.</p><p begin="00:08:12.74" data-timestamp="492.74">And in the same way that when a man goes through a midlife crisis, he might find himself a trophy girlfriend, some 20-year-old who will spike up his hair and put him in ill-fitting T-shirts that have sparkly dragon skeletons on them.
