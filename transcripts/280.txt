Don't get shot.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:49:08.96" data-timestamp="2948.96"> Oh, man. By the way, were you ever scared?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>     Rob  Miller</h4><p begin="00:49:11.89" data-timestamp="2951.89"> Well, to a certain degree, yeah. You're definitely freaked out at certain times. The first night we were moving forward, and it's probably the only time I was ever scared for my company and my battalion, we were pushing forward. It's pitch black. There's no moon. And it's raining, and it's drizzling.</p><p begin="00:49:35.01" data-timestamp="2975.01">And it's 40 degrees out. And everybody's freezing. And my unit's pushing forward. And it's just contact the whole way.</p><p begin="00:49:45.22" data-timestamp="2985.22">And all of a sudden, we get the call over the radio, we got a Marine down. We got a Marine down. We got IEDs in this alley. An IED just went off. So two Marines, they went in to get this guy. Boom. Friggin', these two other guys go down. So now they got three guys in this alley that they think is-- the whole alley is IEDs. And that was the big fear, is that there were just going to be IEDs all across the city. I was like, oh my God. We're going to take so many casualties. It's going to take forever for us to move. Can we do it? Sure. But it's going to be absolutely horrible.</p><p begin="00:50:22.08" data-timestamp="3022.08">At the same time, 3rd platoon had massive contact further down to the east. 1st platoon was actually held up, because they were in a massive engagement to our flank, far east. Bravo Company was just shooting all across their line. And I was like, how can they-- they wanted us to push all the way to the Hidra mosque that night. And I was like, how can they expect us to push to the Hidra mosque.</p><p begin="00:50:49.67" data-timestamp="3049.67">It's totally non-feasible. We got three Marines that are down, and they are wounded. And it was two, three minutes later that the platoon commander came back on. He's like, it wasn't IEDs. It wasn't IEDs. There's a guy in a second story, and he's frickin' throwing hand grenades in this alley.</p><p begin="00:51:10.36" data-timestamp="3070.36">And so they just light this guy up with everything they own. They're throwing the kitchen sink up at this guy. They finally kill this guy.</p><p begin="00:51:18.81" data-timestamp="3078.81">And then the same time, our tanks that were attached to us, Panzer, these guys call us. And he's actually on the road facing east. And he's like, when he calls, you can hear his 240 Golf shooting, and his 50-cal shooting. And he's just like, oh my God. He's like, you need to get somebody else down here. He's like, there's 50 to 60 people retreating from the north to south. And they're just flowing across the road. He's like I can't kill them all.</p><p begin="00:51:49.27" data-timestamp="3109.27">And he must have killed 30, 35 people. And he didn't, he couldn't even-- I don't even know if he made a dent. They were just flowing to the south away from us.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:51:59.77" data-timestamp="3119.77"> This is your second time in Iraq, correct?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Rob Miller</h4><p begin="00:52:02.44" data-timestamp="3122.44"> Yeah.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:52:04.10" data-timestamp="3124.1"> Yeah, and the first time, you didn't see combat, anything like this, right?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4> Rob Miller</h4><p begin="00:52:08.71" data-timestamp="3128.71"> No, no, no. It was pretty cool. My platoon was the first Marine unit on the deck in Mosul. And so we got there expecting to get off and get into a firefight. But it wasn't like that. It wasn't anything like Fallujah.</p><p begin="00:52:28.19" data-timestamp="3148.19">It wasn't really out of control. As soon as we got there, CNN showed up. And they were like, the Marines are on the deck in Mosul. And then everything quieted down.</p><p begin="00:52:39.05" data-timestamp="3159.05">Everybody was like, oh, US forces are here, good to go. Everything's good. Go home, relax. And I did a lot of missions where I would go and just stand on the road and wave at cars. And I really felt like I was trying to collect money. Money for the poor. And I'm out there with a gun.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:53:00.04" data-timestamp="3180.04"> Now that you've seen combat, would you want to go into it again? Or do you feel like you're cured?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4> Rob  Miller</h4><p begin="00:53:06.63" data-timestamp="3186.63"> Yeah. It's a good question. Some people definitely are, they're like, that's it, we're done. But I don't know. It's a tough thing. It's really tough.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:53:21.05" data-timestamp="3201.05"> Well, now that you--</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Rob Miller</h4><p begin="00:53:22.46" data-timestamp="3202.46"> I will say that now that I've been in pretty intense combat, you could say, I'm like, that's good to go. We need to [UNINTELLIGIBLE] dragons, as we say. We're good. We need to get our job done and get home.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:53:37.93" data-timestamp="3217.93"> Yeah. Right. Did you ever get the boxes we sent you, of food?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Rob Miller</h4><p begin="00:53:43.12" data-timestamp="3223.12"> Oh, I got them. Yeah, the artichoke hearts? Yeah, that was great.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:53:48.14" data-timestamp="3228.14"> It was mostly junk food.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Rob Miller</h4><p begin="00:53:49.81" data-timestamp="3229.81"> Oh my God. And those artichoke hearts, I was laughing. I was laughing to tears. And a foreign observer came in, this lieutenant, and he's like, no way. I love artichoke hearts. And I was like, well you are in luck, Neville. And I was like, because I've got a whole jar of artichoke hearts that's got your name on it. And he was like, oh yeah.</p><p begin="00:54:09.93" data-timestamp="3249.93">But we get a lot of good stuff. We get tons of stuff from kids in schools. And we get a lot of crayon drawings that are, you know. The marine is this huge person. And the Iraqis these little small people. And we're throwing hand grenades. And they're like, run away.</p><p begin="00:54:28.18" data-timestamp="3268.18">We get some, though. Especially when you get into middle school and high school, then you get the political kids. The funniest one that comes to my mind was from some youth group out of New York or something. They sent us a bunch of stuff. A couple of them were like, I think President Bush is crazy, and you got to be kidding me, and what are we doing over there.</p><p begin="00:54:47.06" data-timestamp="3287.06">[LAUGHING]</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack   Hitt</h4><p begin="00:54:47.66" data-timestamp="3287.66"> So listen, I have a joke for you. I love Black Hawks. But you know who hates them? Iraqi sheep farmers.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Rob Miller</h4><p begin="00:55:01.50" data-timestamp="3301.5"> Iraqi sheep farmers?</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:55:03.55" data-timestamp="3303.55"> Yeah.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Rob Miller</h4><p begin="00:55:04.62" data-timestamp="3304.62"> Iraqi sheep farmers hate Black Hawks. I don't get it.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:55:10.21" data-timestamp="3310.21"> OK so also on this program is this comic who's been touring in Fallujah. His name is Tom Irwin.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Rob Miller</h4><p begin="00:55:19.05" data-timestamp="3319.05"> Oh yeah, no, he was out here, and all the pogues got to watch him, because everybody else was in the field. That was pretty funny.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:55:24.46" data-timestamp="3324.46"> Oh, you didn't see him.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4> Rob    Miller</h4><p begin="00:55:25.99" data-timestamp="3325.99"> No. We were in Fallujah. I don't know. It burns up all my boys. And they always get ticked off at that kind of stuff. They're like, why are they doing a show for them? They're not getting shot at.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:55:38.25" data-timestamp="3338.25"> Wow, well, we can't wait to see you here back home.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Rob Miller</h4><p begin="00:55:43.82" data-timestamp="3343.82"> I can't wait to get back, see my little boy.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack  Hitt</h4><p begin="00:55:46.09" data-timestamp="3346.09"> Yeah. All right, man. Well, listen, thanks again for all this--</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Rob Miller</h4><p begin="00:55:49.84" data-timestamp="3349.84"> --talking to you.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Jack Hitt</h4><p begin="00:55:51.07" data-timestamp="3351.07"> --al this time, yeah.
