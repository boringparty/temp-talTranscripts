Many of their clients drive from hours away. Small building, mint green roof, unmissable, super bright pink exterior, like the building is saying, yeah, we're here, deal with it.</p><p begin="00:01:43.27" data-timestamp="103.27">Recently, they haven't allowed press inside to record, but we teamed up with a filmmaker, Maisie Crow, who filmed in the clinic starting in 2012 for a great and very nuanced movie that she made about abortion in Mississippi, and is back there now shooting another film.   <h3>Act One: The Pink House</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Maisie Crow</h4><p begin="00:02:50.70" data-timestamp="170.7"> I'm Maisie Crow. This is Act One, The Pink House at the Center of the World. Where would they go?</p><p begin="00:03:14.54" data-timestamp="194.54">We begin this story a couple of days before the Supreme Court decision on a Wednesday night. Because their days are so busy and full, this was the first time they sat down to really discuss what to do as a group if <i>Roe</i> were overturned. The director of the clinic, Shannon Brewer, gathered her staff in the waiting room at the end of the workday after patients had gone home.</p><p begin="00:03:38.06" data-timestamp="218.06">This kind of meeting with everybody together, they're not all that common at the clinic. Everybody's just too busy. So it felt serious.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Shannon Brewer</h4><p begin="00:03:46.25" data-timestamp="226.25"> Y'all going to need to know what's gonna be probably going on within the next week. I don't know.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Maisie Crow</h4><p begin="00:03:57.32" data-timestamp="237.32"> Shannon has worked at the Jackson clinic for more than two decades. Now she's 50 and the leader.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Shannon Brewer</h4><p begin="00:04:17.33" data-timestamp="257.33"> We already know this, that it's going to be crazy. No matter what was thrown at the Pink House-- and that's what everybody calls it-- it was unshuttable. It would not stop, just like Shannon Brewer.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Shannon Brewer</h4><p begin="00:05:12.08" data-timestamp="312.08"> OK, one group there, one group there, six surgicals. Six surgicals. Mm.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Maisie Crow</h4><p begin="00:05:19.13" data-timestamp="319.13"> She's rarely fazed by anything, talks to herself while she works. I've never been in her office when her eyes aren't darting to the screen every few seconds.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Maisie Crow</h4><p begin="00:05:56.08" data-timestamp="356.08"> And what about the sign on your desk?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Shannon Brewer</h4><p begin="00:05:58.46" data-timestamp="358.46"> Oh, don't play with me. That's just the truth. I'm a fucking professional. What are you talking about? That is what it says.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Miki</h4><p begin="00:06:05.39" data-timestamp="365.39"> What I love about that is that it faces inward.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Maisie Crow</h4><p begin="00:06:07.61" data-timestamp="367.61"> That's my producer, Miki.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Shannon Brewer</h4><p begin="00:06:09.65" data-timestamp="369.65"> I know. I'm a fucking professional.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Miki</h4><p begin="00:06:16.67" data-timestamp="376.67"> How awesome would it be as, like, your press conference that you give, whenever the Supreme Court decision is, you just have this right here?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Shannon Brewer</h4><p begin="00:06:22.94" data-timestamp="382.94"> I may just do that.
