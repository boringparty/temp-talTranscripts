Tom Santa</h4><p begin="00:02:20.76" data-timestamp="140.76"> That's right. Right. Yeah. Tom Santa</h4><p begin="00:02:27.45" data-timestamp="147.45"> I responded by I think I told her to pick the tomato soup. [LAUGHS]. I just made the decision.</p><p begin="00:02:34.25" data-timestamp="154.25">And I was young. I was newly ordained. I didn't realize anything. I didn't realize anything more than that she seemingly was asking me a question.</p><p begin="00:02:44.34" data-timestamp="164.34">It wasn't until later, when I was talking to other priests, that I realized that I had encountered my first scrupulous person and that my answer was, in fact, not very pastoral, but pretty flippant. Tom Santa</h4><p begin="00:03:07.68" data-timestamp="187.68"> Until the next time she opened the cabinet, yes.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:03:11.12" data-timestamp="191.12">Oh, good. Tom Santa</h4><p begin="00:03:32.36" data-timestamp="212.36"> Anybody who goes to church, it's about an hour before you receive communion. But anyways, if they  have scrupulosity, they'll be concerned that for instance, as they're sitting there in church, they discovered that they had a seed left in their mouth and they swallowed it. Or things like that.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:03:54.80" data-timestamp="234.8">Yeah.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Fr. Tom Santa</h4><p begin="00:03:55.36" data-timestamp="235.36"> Another one I get commonly is, we're going to go on a cruise and there's no guarantee that we'll have Sunday Mass. So am I committing a mortal sin by skipping Sunday Mass?</p><p begin="00:04:06.13" data-timestamp="246.13">I said no, you're on a boat, and that's what you do. Enjoy your trip. Have a great time. Tom Santa</h4><p begin="00:04:39.67" data-timestamp="279.67">There's a lot of people who make lots of money with that ability.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:04:44.75" data-timestamp="284.75"> Father Santa says that there are two different kinds of scrupulosity. He says that the only thing that can help these people is therapy, behavior modification, drugs.</p><p begin="00:05:53.92" data-timestamp="353.92">And Father Santa says that unfortunately, these are not people who usually go for that. The ones who refuse sometimes tell him that he's just trying to let them off too easy for their sins.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Fr.
