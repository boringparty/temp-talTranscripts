    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:00:00.55" data-timestamp="0.55"> Probably the strangest thing about the K&amp;R business-- K&amp;R, that's Kidnap and Ransom-- is that it's a business at all, that there are enough kidnappings in the world that a whole industry issues kidnap insurance and there's responders who will help your family through the kidnapping and ransom process. And it's also workaday, like any other business.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Daniel Johnson</h4><p begin="00:00:18.91" data-timestamp="18.91"> My name is Daniel Johnson. For instance, he was telling me how K&amp;R insurance works.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Daniel Johnson</h4><p begin="00:00:41.90" data-timestamp="41.9"> So if you were to have a Kidnap Response policy with traveler's insurance and something goes bump in the night, it's our company that gets that call.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:00:48.94" data-timestamp="48.94"> By bump in the night, Dan means what you or I might call a violent, life-threatening, life-changing abduction. See? Understatement. Another interesting fact about kidnapping insurance with this company-- one condition of having the policy is that you don't tell anybody you have the policy. It's like the first rule of Fight Club.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:01:08.79" data-timestamp="68.79"> OK, so let's say that I'm thinking about moving my radio show to a country where there are lots of kidnappings. What should I do?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Daniel Johnson</h4><p begin="00:01:22.00" data-timestamp="82"> Well, most of the advice is going to be somewhat region or geographic specific. Because of the Catholic culture in Mexico, we do recommend that the victims personalize themselves. Ask for a Bible.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:01:42.91" data-timestamp="102.91"> In the Mideast, he says, don't do this or talk about religion. But in Mexico, where there are lots of kidnappings these days--</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Daniel Johnson</h4><p begin="00:01:49.87" data-timestamp="109.87"> It helps personalize you as some-- it's a basis of relation between you and the abductors.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:01:55.63" data-timestamp="115.63"> And it's to my advantage if they see me as a person, why?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Daniel Johnson</h4><p begin="00:01:59.62" data-timestamp="119.62"> Contrary to popular belief and some of the myths, kidnappers, in most cases, don't wantonly, with no reason whatsoever, go in and abuse the victims. Well, the more personal you are to them, the more that they can relate to you, the less likely that's going to happen, the better care that they're going to give.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:02:19.73" data-timestamp="139.73"> The other advantage of a Bible, Dan says, is that you might be captive for a long time. So it's important to keep your mind active, to create a routine of things that you do every day, not to sleep all day, which lots of people end up doing. And if you have a Bible, you can read, which is huge.</p><p begin="00:02:43.94" data-timestamp="163.94">Other tips-- eat what they give you because it's probably the same food that they're eating. Stay with us.</p><p begin="00:04:49.57" data-timestamp="289.57"></p></div>  </div>

  <div class="act-inner">
