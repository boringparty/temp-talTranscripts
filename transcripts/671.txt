And I'm told, hey, we got money.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ben Calhoun</h4><p begin="00:48:31.10" data-timestamp="2911.1"> It was the Democratic operative in DC. "We got money" meant some donors had decided to fund Matt's proposal. Matt's reaction?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Matt Osborne</h4><p begin="00:48:39.92" data-timestamp="2919.92"> Bam.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ben Calhoun</h4><p begin="00:48:41.51" data-timestamp="2921.51"> No kidding. This is for real?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Matt Osborne</h4><p begin="00:48:43.08" data-timestamp="2923.08"> Oh, wow. This is real. This is-- this is happening. OK, cool.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ben Calhoun</h4><p begin="00:48:48.90" data-timestamp="2928.9"> The donors put up $100,000. Part of that would pay for a team of people to create these fake conservative groups. The rest of the money, most of it, would pay for ads on Facebook to shove what they made into the social media feeds of Alabama Republicans.</p><p begin="00:49:04.85" data-timestamp="2944.85">At this point, there were only 17 days left before the election. The team doing this, I should say-- sort of fascinatingly tiny-- just two people in DC and then Matt in Alabama.</p><p begin="00:49:23.64" data-timestamp="2963.64">One of the people in DC, Matt says, was sort of doing oversight, not even super involved. The other person in DC created tweets and memes. Matt did the Facebook posts and the videos.</p><p begin="00:49:34.72" data-timestamp="2974.72">The whole group was so small. It kind of makes you realize, you don't need a building full of people to do this kind of thing. It can be a conspiracy of just two or three.</p><p begin="00:49:50.49" data-timestamp="2990.49">Matt, as an Alabama native, was kind of the person who the group turned to make the con seem authentic. How would they say that?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Matt Osborne</h4><p begin="00:49:58.65" data-timestamp="2998.65"> Hey, Matt, how do you spell "barbecue" in Alabama? Alabamian instead of Alabaman, that sort of thing.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ben Calhoun</h4><p begin="00:50:19.61" data-timestamp="3019.61"> There were some things that were deemed too much, even by dirty tactic standards. Matt pointed out that something that would inevitably get a ton of engagement would be an AR-15 giveaway. The funder, it turned out, was not OK with a gun giveaway, real or fake. But even if they weren't going to give away an AR-15, there was a lot of room for imagination. Like, Matt recruited a woman he knew.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Matt Osborne</h4><p begin="00:50:43.00" data-timestamp="3043"> I will just-- for our purposes today, I will call her Sharon.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ben Calhoun</h4><p begin="00:50:45.97" data-timestamp="3045.97"> Matt hired Sharon to do a voiceover on a video that he made for Dry Alabama.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Matt Osborne</h4><p begin="00:50:51.33" data-timestamp="3051.33"> She's wonderful. Altogether, $51 million was spent on the race, which unleashed a hurricane of TV ads, armies of canvassers, meaning it's just impossible to gauge what effect Matt's thing had.</p><p begin="00:53:10.23" data-timestamp="3190.23">Matt's team considered the project a success, though. That was part of what they were testing.</p><p begin="00:53:34.49" data-timestamp="3214.49">I'm just going to confess here that I'm somebody who's troubled by the culture of our politics right now. And that's bad for everybody.</p><p begin="00:53:54.17" data-timestamp="3234.17">Maybe because of that, when I went to see Matt, I half thought that because he spent so much time outraged about this kind of deceptive and scuzzy politics, that maybe, after having some time to think it over, he'd end up with some kind of buyer's remorse. Like, I drank so much. What did I say?</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ben Calhoun</h4><p begin="00:54:10.98" data-timestamp="3250.98"> Doesn't it feel like, especially in-- you know, during the presidency of Donald Trump, that the norms are only the norms if most people adhere to them, you know? Like--</p></div><div class="subject"><h4> Matt  Osborne</h4><p begin="00:54:27.87" data-timestamp="3267.87"> Well, what is the norm here? The norm is that Republicans play dirty and win.</p><p begin="00:54:45.15" data-timestamp="3285.15">Is that the norm that we're supposed to preserve? Burn it to the ground.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ben Calhoun</h4><p begin="00:54:56.75" data-timestamp="3296.75"> So the core of what I hear you saying is that you don't think that it's something that you can combat by example and just say, we're going to refuse to win that way.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Matt Osborne</h4><p begin="00:55:07.12" data-timestamp="3307.12"> Oh, look at me. In another, a company microtargeted voters on social media, sent them messages it claims drove up Democratic turnout by 4% and drove down Republican turnout by over 2%. Senator Doug Jones, I should say, has said he disapproves, knew nothing about this stuff. He's called for an investigation.</p><p begin="00:56:15.37" data-timestamp="3375.37">From talking to Democratic fundraisers, I can tell you for sure, there's plenty of donors who don't want any part of this kind of thing. But there's definitely a portion, I hear, who say, yeah, it's time. They're ready to throw money at stuff like this.</p><p begin="00:56:29.95" data-timestamp="3389.95">Matt says, he thinks, when it comes to these kind of dirty tactics, the situation is going to have to get worse before it gets better, because in our gridlocked system, you won't get regulations against this stuff until both parties are fed up with it, which, that sounds like a real bummer to me. But I have to admit, there's a logic to that.</p><p begin="00:56:50.65" data-timestamp="3410.65">For his part, Matt's not going to stop, at least if someone will pay for him to keep doing things like this. He wants to get hired to do more stuff just like this.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira  Glass</h4><p begin="00:57:10.57" data-timestamp="3430.57"> Ben Calhoun is one of the producers of our show.</p></div>  </div>
