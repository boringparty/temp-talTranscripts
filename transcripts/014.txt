It's alive.</p><p begin="00:01:22.79" data-timestamp="82.79">You feel like you're hearing these people as they really are even though there is a tape recorder and a boom microphone, a huge one, a foot and a half long, and a stranger. It was intimate. And it completely revealed what the people speaking were like.</p><p begin="00:02:20.73" data-timestamp="140.73">And hearing that tape gave us the idea for today's program, that we're going to devote the whole show to three stories made out of found tape like this.   <h3>Act One: Berrien Springs, Michigan</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:02:56.71" data-timestamp="176.71">Act One, Berrien Springs, Michigan. The tape that Joe Silovsky found is from 1967. It's nine-inch reel of tape, two hours of tape.</p><p begin="00:03:19.31" data-timestamp="199.31">And the box had stamps all over it, and addresses written, and crossed out, and written. I had it sent right direct to you. It isn't that I think you need it. It's just a new insight on mental health, mental illness, that I think is good.</p><p begin="00:04:17.35" data-timestamp="257.35">I probably told you about the chapter I read to Jimmy when we were riding to Mrs. Lester's voice lesson. And here, it brought out that sometimes a person nags because they want to feel superior.</p><p begin="00:04:56.56" data-timestamp="296.56">Each time they-- well, here, I'll read it, son. &quot;If you stop to think about it, you know perfectly well that this sort of nagging, that means nagging to get things done and things, isn't correcting the evil of what you complain. So for goodness sake, find some more constructive way of enhancing your feeling of virtue if it's so badly in need of being enhanced.&quot;</p><p begin="00:05:31.79" data-timestamp="331.79">Daddy has been very, very busy working in the basement. And we are out of extrusions and won't be having them until Friday.</p><p begin="00:05:54.77" data-timestamp="354.77">I made $50 in one week and a half. That's when I bought-- I bought your suit, and paid my tithe, and things like this. Well, son, I'll say goodbye. But thank you for your words of advice as far as my resting. And thank you for being concerned about my welfare.</p><p begin="00:06:10.40" data-timestamp="370.4">I really have gotten along quite well recently. I didn't feel too bad of depression last month. We don't have any strips right now though. I guess I got a call for supper, so I'll come back later.</p><p begin="00:06:55.95" data-timestamp="415.95">I heard a little story that I thought I would tell you, Joe. Mom reminded me at the supper table. I just got back from eating supper. There were a couple out. They were getting acquainted, shall we say, doing a little French kissing. And the fella said, &quot;Oh, I think I got your gum.&quot; And she said, &quot;No.&quot; She said, &quot;I got a bad cold.&quot; So I'll leave you with that little thought for the time being.</p><p begin="00:07:39.46" data-timestamp="459.46">Hi, Joe. I just got through talking to you on the phone. Little Kenny [? Jacques, ?] who I don't know if you remember him or not, but he's Quint [? Jacques's ?] oldest son, got married last March. And his girlfriend was here in school. And they were intending to keep it quiet until after school was out, but I guess he's pregnant now. So I suppose when you fool around, and do things like that, why, I suppose that's the price you're prepared to take. But I guess he's still in the Army.</p><p begin="00:08:10.09" data-timestamp="490.09">I saw him tonight in the drive-in. They look real happy together. She seems like a real nice little girl, almost too nice for him. He's a real nice fella, but he's quite mentally retarded, I think. Anyway, that's the way it goes.</p><p begin="00:08:34.31" data-timestamp="514.31">And she seems to be real happy with him. She went with Carl Hamel before. And she seems like a good Christian girl. She's got one hand that's deformed. I suppose that makes her feel like it'd be awfully for her to get anybody. So she probably thought she'd take whatever she could get.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Mother</h4><p begin="00:09:03.13" data-timestamp="543.13">It was interesting as we visited Aunt Menta and Uncle Wendall's this weekend. And I realized that it's because many times I perhaps force myself on him. So I didn't say anything until about 50 miles before we got there.</p><p begin="00:09:34.39" data-timestamp="574.39">I really felt as though Menta should know we were coming. I should have written and told her. But I've left the communication with his family to Daddy. Well, he got a little bit vexed, and jumped out of the car, and went to the telephone, saying, &quot;I hope you're satisfied now.&quot; But when he came back, Menta hadn't realized we were coming. And we were all grateful that we'd made the telephone call even though I didn't want to be right and say, &quot;I told you so.&quot; Because it wouldn't have been nice to have walked in on them at a quarter to 10:00 at night.</p><p begin="00:10:08.53" data-timestamp="608.53">It was interesting how genuinely they accepted us. I don't know if I could be that gracious or not. Although they live such different lives. They had no plans for the weekend. We came. This was fine. And we ate what they had. And this was it.</p><p begin="00:10:25.62" data-timestamp="625.62">But I think of if the situation were reversed and Ment and Wendall dropped in on us, how many times we have company over the weekend, we have to go to functions, and we have music lessons, and things like this, and our lives are so completely different.
