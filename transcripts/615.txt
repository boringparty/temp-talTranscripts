So what's he like?</p></div><div class="subject"><h4> Reid Cherlin</h4><p begin="00:00:27.25" data-timestamp="27.25"> He was very engaging. And he's this happy warrior type. There's this positivity about him.</p><p begin="00:00:55.87" data-timestamp="55.87">So he sat down and he basically just started talking. Yeah.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4> Steve  Bannon</h4><p begin="00:01:23.11" data-timestamp="83.11"> Well, we try to make sure the content has the swagger to it. It has a point of view. These right-wing agitators with their far-out ideas.</p><p begin="00:04:45.50" data-timestamp="285.5">And listen to what seemed completely irrelevant 2 and 1/2 years ago. He looked kind of out of place. And I just thought, why is he here?</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:05:41.12" data-timestamp="341.12"> In fact, he and Bannon, and his staff and the Breitbart staff knew each other. Reid headed out to the backyard.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Reid Cherlin</h4><p begin="00:05:58.80" data-timestamp="358.8"> Talking to some of the Breitbart writers who were smoking cigarettes with Nigel Farage, head of the UK Independence Party, who was telling me about leaving the European Union, which I didn't think sounded particularly likely. And I thought maybe Trump's off-the-cuff, not-deeply-informed-on-the-issues speaking style would be repellent to him, but he said no. He admires the spontaneity. The catchphrases.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Pat Buchanan</h4><p begin="00:49:15.45" data-timestamp="2955.45"> Like, "and Mexico's going to pay for it." It was a very effective speech. It's a long shot, but it is a chance.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:50:37.82" data-timestamp="3037.82"> When Buchanan says it's the last chance for these ideas, what he means is the country's problems have gotten so bad. In a number of books, he's written with alarm about America becoming a less white, more diverse country. "We are so divided already," he says, "and this divides us more."</p><p begin="00:51:01.26" data-timestamp="3061.26">There's another one of his ideas that's come back in the age of Trump, championed by Steve Bannon and Jeff Sessions and a world of radio talk shows and websites and Breitbart News. I don't see it now.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:53:33.61" data-timestamp="3213.61"> There's another way that Trump represents the last chance for Buchanan's ideas. And this is something really interesting about where Republicans are right now, even in this moment of triumph they're having, where they have the White House and both houses of Congress and so many state houses and governor's mansions around the country.</p><p begin="00:53:57.93" data-timestamp="3237.93">When they look at the future, they're still looking down the barrel of the same dilemma they had in 2014, back at the beginning of now. The party is still wrestling with the same issue that got Eric Cantor knocked out of office. They have to choose between two policies, neither of which seems like it solves their national political problems. If they stay tough on immigration, they think they'll kiss off most of the Latino vote, which will doom them. Though, if they provide a path to citizenship, they believe those new Latino voters mostly will be Democrats, and that'll doom them.</p></div><div class="interviewer"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:54:27.41" data-timestamp="3267.41"> So what do you think the Republican Party should do? It's not white nationalism, he says.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Pat Buchanan</h4><p begin="00:54:57.45" data-timestamp="3297.45"> You can't have white nationalists if they're diminishing as a share of the population. And if this doesn't work, the now that we're in right now-- the whole "Donald Trump, America first, reject the rest of the world, wall up the borders" America-- that will not be the future, for the country or the party. It'll just be a blip.</p><p begin="00:55:45.21" data-timestamp="3345.21">Pat Buchanan's newest book, by the way, is history that he participated in himself. The candidate's telling the crowd that, even though he didn't win the Republican nomination, his ideas about America first, they still had a future.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Pat Buchanan</h4><p begin="00:56:08.82" data-timestamp="3368.82"> Before our eyes and before their eyes, this party is becoming a Buchanan Party. The old era is over. The old order--</p><p begin="00:56:17.94" data-timestamp="3377.94">[APPLAUDING WILDLY]</p><p begin="00:56:23.77" data-timestamp="3383.77">The old order is passing away. But within this party, a new party is being born. God willing, God willing, we will be there at its birth, and one day, the stone the builders rejected shall become the cornerstone.</p><p begin="00:56:51.86" data-timestamp="3411.86">[MUSIC - "HOW IT STARTS" BY CAMEO CULTURE]</p></div>  </div>
