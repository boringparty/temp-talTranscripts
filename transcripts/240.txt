<div class="act" id="act1">
  <h3>Act One: It's Not Just The Aces That Are Wild</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:05:40.62" data-timestamp="340.62">And let's get right to it with Act One. Here's writer David Sedaris, recorded in front of a live audience in Boston.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>David Sedaris</h4><p begin="00:05:48.80" data-timestamp="348.8">My parents were not the type of people who went to bed at a regular hour. Sleep overtook them, but neither the time nor the idea of a mattress seemed very important. My father favored a chair in the basement, but my mother was apt to lie down anywhere, waking with carpet burns on her face or the pattern of the sofa embossed into the soft flesh of her upper arms. It was sort of embarrassing. She might sleep for eight hours a day. But they were never consecutive hours, and they involved no separate outfit.</p><p begin="00:06:26.30" data-timestamp="386.3">For Christmas, we would give her nightgowns, hoping she might take the hint. They're for bedtime, we'd say.
