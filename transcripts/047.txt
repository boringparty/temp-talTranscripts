  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:04:41.27" data-timestamp="281.27">Act Two.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>David Sedaris</h4><p begin="00:04:42.37" data-timestamp="282.37">My costume is green.
