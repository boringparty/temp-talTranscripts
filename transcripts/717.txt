It was because of where I saw it.</p><p begin="00:00:55.93" data-timestamp="55.93">I was on vacation. We didn't take many vacations when I was a kid, but on one of them, we stayed at this hotel in Florida where the rooms had this thing where they offered a couple movies all day long.
