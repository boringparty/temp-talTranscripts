  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="subject"><h4>Andrew Forsthoefel</h4><p begin="00:03:58.34" data-timestamp="238.34">OK, so October 14, 2011, having my last breakfast at home. I feel my chest right now just racing. Oh, man. I couldn't believe the coincidence.</p><p begin="00:05:08.24" data-timestamp="308.24">It's not a coincidence, he said. Your mom's a wreck back at the house. I'm walking across America.</p><p begin="00:06:10.42" data-timestamp="370.42">I followed them into the woods. And their camp was behind a shopping center I'd been to a lot.
