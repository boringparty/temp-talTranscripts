Less so is he amenable to it when those strangers are asking him questions. In Russia when Sergei was young, it happened plenty. The KGB felt right at home knocking on his door. His father had been a Zionist, which was pretty much an invitation for them to drop by any old time.</p><p begin="00:46:56.30" data-timestamp="2816.3">When Sergei got to Israel and then moved to Jaffa, his family couldn't wrap their heads around it. They'd ask him, &quot;What are you looking to find in a place like that? There's no one there but addicts and Arabs and pensioners.&quot; But what is most excellent about addicts and Arabs and pensioners is that they don't come around knocking on Sergei's door. Like that, Sergei can get his sleep and get up when it's still dark. He can take his little boat out into the sea and fish until he's done fishing, by himself, in silence. The way it should be. The way it was.</p><p begin="00:47:35.71" data-timestamp="2855.71">Until one day some kid with an earring in his ear-- looking a little bit homosexual-- comes knocking. Hard like that, rapping at his door, just the way Sergei doesn't like. And he says, this kid, that he has some questions he wants to put on the TV. Sergei tells the boy, tells him in what he thinks is a straightforward manner, that he doesn't want it. Not interested. Sergei gives the camera a shove to help make it clearer. But the earring boy is stubborn. He says all kinds of things, fast things, and it's a bit hard for Sergei to follow. His Hebrew isn't so good. The boy slows it down. Tells Sergei he's got a strong face, a nice face, and that he simply have to have him for this movie picture.</p><p begin="00:48:22.84" data-timestamp="2902.84">Sergei can also slow down. He can also make clear. He tells the kid to [BLEEP] off. But the kid is slippery, and somehow between saying no and pushing the door closed, Sergei finds that the kid is in his house. He's already making his movie, running his camera without any permission. And from behind the camera, still telling Sergei about his face. That it's full of feeling. That it's tender.</p><p begin="00:48:50.52" data-timestamp="2930.52">Suddenly the kid spots Sergei's goldfish flitting around in its big glass jar in his kitchen. The kid with the earring starts screaming goldfish, goldfish. He's so excited. And this, this really pressures Sergei, who tells the kid, &quot;It's nothing. Just a regular goldfish. Stop filming it. Just a goldfish,&quot; Sergei tells him. Just something he found flapping around in the net, a deep sea goldfish.</p><p begin="00:49:16.93" data-timestamp="2956.93">But the boy isn't listening. He's still filming, and getting closer, and saying something about talking and fish and a magic wish. Sergei doesn't like this. Doesn't like that the boy is almost at it, already reaching for the jar.</p><p begin="00:49:33.19" data-timestamp="2973.19">In this instance, Sergei understands the boy didn't come for television. What he came for specifically is to snatch Sergei's fish, to steal it away.</p><p begin="00:49:48.42" data-timestamp="2988.42">Before the mind of Sergei [? Garalik ?] really understands what it is his body has done, he seems to have taken the burner off the stove and hit the boy in the head. The boy falls. The camera falls with him. The camera breaks open on the floor, along with the boy's skull. There's a lot of blood coming out of that head. And Sergei really doesn't know what to do.</p><p begin="00:50:14.55" data-timestamp="3014.55">That is, he knows exactly what to do, but it really would complicate things. Because if he brings this kid to the hospital, people are going to ask what happened. And it would take things in a direction Sergei doesn't want to go.</p><p begin="00:50:31.13" data-timestamp="3031.13">&quot;No reason to take him to the hospital anyway,&quot; says the goldfish in Russian. &quot;That one's already dead.&quot;</p><p begin="00:50:38.28" data-timestamp="3038.28">&quot;He can't be dead,&quot; Sergei says with a moan. &quot;I barely popped him. It's only a burner, only a little thing.&quot; Sergei holds it up to the fish, taps it against his own skull to prove it. It's not even that hard.</p><p begin="00:50:51.99" data-timestamp="3051.99">&quot;Maybe not,&quot; says the fish. &quot;But apparently, it's harder than that kid's head.&quot;</p><p begin="00:50:57.62" data-timestamp="3057.62">&quot;He wanted to take you from me,&quot; Sergei says almost crying.</p><p begin="00:51:01.82" data-timestamp="3061.82">&quot;Nonsense,&quot; the fish says. &quot;He was only here to make a little nothing for TV.&quot;</p><p begin="00:51:06.37" data-timestamp="3066.37">&quot;But he said--&quot;</p><p begin="00:51:07.31" data-timestamp="3067.31">&quot;He said,&quot; says the fish interrupting, &quot;exactly what he was doing but you didn't get it. Honestly, your Hebrew, it's terrible.&quot;</p><p begin="00:51:15.89" data-timestamp="3075.89">&quot;Yours is better?&quot; Sergei says. &quot;Yours is so great?&quot;</p><p begin="00:51:19.71" data-timestamp="3079.71">&quot;Yes, mine's super great,&quot; the goldfish says, sounding impatient. &quot;I'm a magic fish. I'm fluent in everything.&quot;</p><p begin="00:51:30.30" data-timestamp="3090.3">All the while, the puddle of blood from the earring kid's head is getting bigger and bigger, and Sergei is on his toes up against the kitchen wall desperate not to step in it, to get blood on his feet.</p><p begin="00:51:40.60" data-timestamp="3100.6">&quot;You do have one wish left,&quot; the fish reminds Sergei. He says it easy like that, as if Sergei doesn't know. As is either of them ever loses count.</p><p begin="00:51:50.98" data-timestamp="3110.98">&quot;No,&quot; Sergei says. Saving it for something.&quot;</p><p begin="00:51:59.11" data-timestamp="3119.11">&quot;For what?&quot; the fish says. But Sergei won't answer.</p><p begin="00:52:09.52" data-timestamp="3129.52">That first wish Sergei used when they discovered a cancer in his sister. A lung cancer. The kind you don't get better from. The kid was still small then, barely three, but the doctors already knew something in her son's head wasn't right. He was going to grow big, but not in the brain. Three was about as smart as he'd get. Svetia cried to Sergei in bed all night. Sergei walked home along the beach when the sun came up and he called to the fish, asked the goldfish to fix it as soon as he'd crossed through the door. He never told Svetia. And a few months later she left him for some cop, a Moroccan with a shiny Honda.</p><p begin="00:52:59.98" data-timestamp="3179.98">In his heart, Sergei kept telling himself it wasn't for Svetia that he'd done it. That he'd wished his wish purely the boy. In his mind, he was less sure. And all kinds of thoughts about other things he could have done with that wish continued to gnaw at him, half driving him mad.</p><p begin="00:53:17.39" data-timestamp="3197.39">The third wish, Sergei hadn't yet wished for.</p><p begin="00:53:23.32" data-timestamp="3203.32">&quot;I can restore him,&quot; says the gold fish. &quot;I can bring him back to life.&quot;</p><p begin="00:53:27.72" data-timestamp="3207.72">&quot;No one's asking,&quot; Sergei says.</p><p begin="00:53:30.60" data-timestamp="3210.6">&quot;I can bring him back to the moment before,&quot; the goldfish says. &quot;To before he knocks on your door. I can put him back to right there. I can do it. All you need to do is ask.&quot;</p><p begin="00:53:41.51" data-timestamp="3221.51">&quot;To wish my wish,&quot; Sergei says. &quot;My last.&quot;</p><p begin="00:53:46.03" data-timestamp="3226.03">The fish swishes his tail back and forth in the water, the way he does, Sergei knows, when he's truly excited. The goldfish can already taste freedom. Sergei can see it on him.</p><p begin="00:53:58.69" data-timestamp="3238.69">After the last wish, Sergei won't have a choice. He'll have to let the goldfish go. His magic goldfish. His friend.</p><p begin="00:54:07.44" data-timestamp="3247.44">&quot;It's fixable,&quot; Sergei says. &quot;I'll just mop up the blood. A good sponge and it will be like it never was.&quot;</p><p begin="00:54:15.04" data-timestamp="3255.04">That tail just goes back and forth. The fish's head steady. Sergei takes a deep breath. He steps out into the middle of the kitchen, out into that puddle.</p><p begin="00:54:26.54" data-timestamp="3266.54">&quot;When I'm fishing, while it's dark and the world's asleep,&quot; he says, half to himself and half to the fish. &quot;I'll tie the kid to a rock and dump him in the sea. Not a chance, not in a million years will anyone ever find him.&quot;</p><p begin="00:54:40.08" data-timestamp="3280.08">&quot;You killed him, Sergei, the goldfish says. &quot;You murdered someone. His name was Munir. He was fat with a big white mustache. Super photogenic. It was moving the way he said it. Perfect, the way in which Munir wished his wish. Yoni knew right while he was filming that this guy would be his promo for sure.</p><p begin="00:55:29.05" data-timestamp="3329.05">Either him or that Russian. The one that looked straight into the camera and said if he ever found a talking goldfish, he wouldn't ask of it a single thing.
