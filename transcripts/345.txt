  <h3>Act One: Fred And Barney</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:06:10.82" data-timestamp="370.82">Act One, Fred and Barney. This first story comes from Jonathan Goldstein and David Rakoff. Fred, I, um-- listen, I don't know how to even tell you this, but I was pulling out of the driveway, and I accidentally ran over Dino. He's dead, Fred. I'm really, really, really sorry. I-- he was sleeping on our driveway behind the car. I just backed up over him. I didn't see him or anything. I hate to be leaving something like this on your answering machine.</p><p begin="00:07:34.04" data-timestamp="454.04">The thing is, Betty and I have to get to the airport because we're supposed to make our flight to Rock-apulco.
