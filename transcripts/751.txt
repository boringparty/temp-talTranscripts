It's part of an entire genre of films, disaster films, that have never gotten any respect. Nobody thinks of these films as art. But I loved it at the time when I saw it. It felt big, and it felt important and serious. And I remember it was very emotional.</p><p begin="00:00:47.23" data-timestamp="47.23">And the reason that I saw it so many times, that this is the film that I saw more than any other, was not because I loved the film. We didn't take many vacations when I was a kid, but on one of them, we stayed at this hotel in Florida where the rooms had this thing where they offered a couple of movies all day long. And this was so long ago in the 1970s. My sisters and I, we had never seen anything like that. Even cable TV was rare back then. <i>The</i> <i>Poseidon</i> <i>Adventure</i> was the film that they offered. And the most likely outcome is that they'll hate you for it.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="01:00:25.32" data-timestamp="3625.32"> Elna Baker is one of the producers of our show. Since we first aired this story, both Will and Rebecca each have gotten married. Rebecca has a new TV show called <i>Guilty</i> <i>Party</i> on Paramount Plus. And Will has a comedy album out. To find it, look for Will Weldon on Amazon Music.</p></div>  </div>

<div class="act" id="act4">
  <h3>Act Four: The Kid Namastays in the Picture</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="01:00:46.73" data-timestamp="3646.73"> Act Four, "The Kid Namastays in the Picture." So our show today is about people who watch a film and then have their own very particular take on it. And not long ago, I learned about somebody like that. Here she is in Harry Potter glasses and a onesie with a Gryffindor crest on it, giving her version of the first Harry Potter film.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Jaime Amor</h4><p begin="01:01:06.74" data-timestamp="3666.74"> We meet Harry, aged 10, living in a house. Coming up to stand, take your feet wide, arms out and above your head, house pose. Harry lives at Number 4 Privet Drive.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="01:01:18.74" data-timestamp="3678.74"> This is Jaime Amor, the host or, I guess, yoga instructor, of a series of videos called Cosmic Kids Yoga. Viewership of these videos skyrocketed during quarantine from about 100,000 viewers a day to about a million. And a lot of her most popular videos for kids are her retelling the stories of children's movies-- <i>Moana</i> or <i>Trolls</i> or <i>Star</i> <i>Wars--</i> and combining the stories with yoga.</p><p begin="01:01:43.43" data-timestamp="3703.43">Back when we first aired today's program at the height of the quarantine, I watched a six-year-old regularly and very happily do yoga for a half hour with these YouTube videos before starting his day of remote learning first grade. I would walk by and I would just get totally caught up in these videos.</p><p begin="01:01:58.10" data-timestamp="3718.1">And I have to say, a lot of my pleasure and complete fascination with them was seeing the ingenuity that Jaime Amor uses to incorporate the poses into the storytelling. Like, for example, warrior pose for Elsa, making snow and ice in <i>Frozen.</i> Forward bends as <i>Alice</i> <i>in</i> <i>Wonderland</i> leans over to take a drink from the bottle that says "drink me" on it. In <i>The</i> <i>Wizard</i> <i>of</i> <i>Oz,</i> when the tornado arrives, Dorothy is on her bed, in bed pose, of course.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Jaime Amor</h4><p begin="01:02:23.78" data-timestamp="3743.78"> Look out the window. There's that mean lady from the village. She's riding a bicycle in the sky. Lying on your backs, criss cross your fingers.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="01:02:33.02" data-timestamp="3753.02"> Bicycle pose, of course. Pada Sanchalanasana.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Jaime Amor</h4><p begin="01:02:35.18" data-timestamp="3755.18"> Lift up your legs and pedal your legs like you're riding a bicycle. She's cackling like a witch. [CACKLES] But she's not riding a bicycle anymore. She's on a broomstick with a black hat. She really is a witch. Coming into broomstick pose, lying on your tummies, everyone.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="01:02:56.33" data-timestamp="3776.33"> This is locust pose, Salabhasana.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Jaime Amor</h4><p begin="01:02:58.21" data-timestamp="3778.21"> Take your arms down by your side, and lift your feet and chest up at the same time, going, whoosh!</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="01:03:06.13" data-timestamp="3786.13"> The videos are actually structured like a real yoga class, always starting with a sitting pose and namaste, and always ending with shavasana, resting pose.</p></div><div class="subject"><h4>Jaime Amor</h4><p begin="01:03:14.77" data-timestamp="3794.77"> Lying all the way down.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="01:03:16.48" data-timestamp="3796.48"> And each of the videos plays a little half-hour yoga appreciation of movie, though they're all kid's films, so Jaime Amor's kid-friendly tone matches them perfectly. But putting together today's program, we wondered what it would be like if she took on a film that's made for grownups and beloved by grownups, not by kids. So we reached out to her and her husband, Martin, who makes these videos with her. And they were into it. And we considered a bunch of different films to turn into yoga-- <i>Silence</i> <i>of</i> <i>the</i> <i>Lambs</i> and <i>Pulp</i> <i>Fiction</i> and <i>Parasite,</i> before we settled on this film. Jaime Amor prepared this for us.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Jaime Amor</h4><p begin="01:03:54.56" data-timestamp="3834.56"> Thelma and Louise are best friends. Let's do a hug pose, wrapping our arms around our shoulders, like we're hugging our best friend. They set out for a weekend away in the mountains to take a break. On the way, they stop for a cold, refreshing drink at a bar. Thelma dances with a man called Harlan. Coming into our dancer pose, standing on one leg, we catch our foot with our hand behind us, and lift it as we reach forwards with our other arm.</p><p begin="01:04:27.62" data-timestamp="3867.62">Harlan wants to do more than dancing with Thelma, but she doesn't want to. Louise finds them and is so angry at Harlan that she shoots him in the chest. Coming into our shooter warrior pose, standing one foot forward, one foot back, we bend our front knee and open our arms wide. Pow! Thelma and Louise whiz off in the car. Coming into car pose, sitting with legs out long, arms forward to hold the steering wheel. Oh, dear. What have they done?</p><p begin="01:05:03.11" data-timestamp="3903.11">On the road, the women meet a handsome, friendly young man called JD. Thelma rather likes JD and invites him for a sleepover. In the morning, JD wakes up. Coming up to stand, reaching our arms up to the sky, we wave and say, hello, sun. And while Thelma and Louise go to breakfast, JD steals all of Louise's money. Oh, dear. Thelma feels really bad. To fix the situation, Thelma decides to become a robber and steal all the money from a nearby market. Naughty Thelma. Now the police are after them.</p><p begin="01:05:47.84" data-timestamp="3947.84">Thelma and Louise arrive at the Grand Canyon. Coming to stand in our mountain pose, standing with our feet hip distance, arms by our side, we become as still and strong as the mountains. They take a breath together, soaking up the incredible view and the peace of this place. All of a sudden, a helicopter thunders into view. Jumping our feet wide and clapping our hands up over our heads. The dust swirls and the sirens scream. Arms wide, we spin, side to side to side. They're trapped.</p><p begin="01:06:33.61" data-timestamp="3993.61">Thelma looks at her best friend. Listen, let's not get caught. Let's keep going. Louise checks if Thelma is sure. She nods. Louise cups Thelma's face and kisses her, cupping our faces to feel our cheeks. Louise steps on the gas, stepping one foot forward. As the car screeches forward, Thelma and Louise clasp hands. The car flies off the cliff. Coming into our flying pose, lifting up our back leg, balancing on our standing leg, and taking our arms out wide, we're flying!</p><p begin="01:07:23.14" data-timestamp="4043.14">After all that action, we're ready to relax now. So we lie down and let our bodies feel heavy. We close our eyes, and take a big deep breath.</p><p begin="01:07:36.43" data-timestamp="4056.43">[INHALES] [EXHALES]</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="01:07:41.71" data-timestamp="4061.71"> Jaime Amor, her yoga videos for kids that mix movies and yoga, but also there are ones which mix video games and yoga and all kinds of other things, are at cosmickids.com. Or find their free YouTube channel, youtube.com/cosmickidsyoga.</p><p begin="01:07:57.73" data-timestamp="4077.73">[MUSIC - "LET'S GO TO THE MOVIES" BY AILEEN QUINN]</p></div>  </div>
