  <h3>Act One: Him Talk Pretty Three Days</h3>
  <div class="act-inner">
    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:06:28.31" data-timestamp="388.31"> Act one, "Him Talk Pretty Three Days." I know I must have a lighter in here. I will not spend the afternoon asking people if they have fire.</p></div><div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:08:02.70" data-timestamp="482.7"> I was curious about David's thoughts about Paris, precisely because he was never somebody who had any special feelings for the place. He didn't move here with his head full of Ernest Hemingway, Gertrude Stein, Sartre, and Proust. He was a blank slate.</p><p begin="00:08:16.43" data-timestamp="496.43">And so for several hours every day I was in Paris, David would walk me to the places he finds most interesting. And the first thing I learned was this.
