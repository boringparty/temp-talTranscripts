    <div class="host"><h4>Ira Glass</h4><p begin="00:46:43.75" data-timestamp="2803.75"> Act Four, The Feels on the Bus. Not for repressed high school kids who'd run alongside the bus and stare at it longingly, and not for high-strung people in windbreakers who'd bang on the door as if they were actually on time, and it was the driver who was out of line.</p><p begin="00:47:24.01" data-timestamp="2844.01">And not even for little old ladies with brown paper bags full of groceries, who struggled to flag him down with trembling hands. Because the seconds would be lost by every single passenger on the bus. And if there were, say, 60 people on the bus who hadn't done anything wrong and had all arrived to the bus stop on time, then together, they'd be losing half an hour, which is double 15 minutes.</p><p begin="00:48:27.59" data-timestamp="2907.59">This was the only reason why he never opened the door.
